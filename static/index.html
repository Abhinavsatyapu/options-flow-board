Signal board

Signals today (newest first)
Bias = full or building scenario read when present, else score from CUM_LEAN side, 10-min CUM_LEAN slope, roll10 sign and price structure (≥3 one way; whale bias and OFI+tape agreement add ±0.5 each when that data is fed). VWAP box = session VWAP from Yahoo 1-min volume, price vs VWAP and the VWAP bias: BULLISH = all four checks bullish, BEARISH = all four bearish, NEUTRAL = building (small text shows e.g. "Bullish building 3/4") or no edge. Tap "Details" on a card for the rule checks, score breakdown and structure. Checks: Break+hold = price holding beyond a broken 5-min swing level; an entry fires only on the hold close with all 5 green and no stay-out. Signals are rule-based, not advice.

Per-minute flow log - SPCX

C/P bot/sold = ATM call/put contracts bought/sold that minute; LEAN = NET C − NET P; Prem net = premium bought − sold; Δ sh = net delta shares; ΣΔ = running sum of Δ sh over today's stored closed minutes. L/S = long/short entry checks passing (of 5). VWAP / vs VWAP / VWAP scen. = session VWAP from Yahoo 1-min volume, last 1-min close vs VWAP, and the VWAP bias at that minute (BULLISH / BEARISH / NEUTRAL; the small text is the read on completed 5-min candles). • = 5-min bar close. "live" row = forming minute, not used for decisions.
